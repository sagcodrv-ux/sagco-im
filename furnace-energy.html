Furnace Energy Consumption — fuel-to-output tracking per furnace, separate from the Furnace Monitoring Logs (temperatures and process readings). One record per furnace per month. It can be filled in two ways: Daily — add a line each day with liters consumed and tons of glass melted, and the month's totals build up as days are added; or Bulk — type the month's total liters and total melted tons directly, in one go. The entry mode is fixed once the record is created. A Daily-mode month can only be closed once the number of daily lines matches the actual number of days in that month (28–31, including leap years); a Bulk-mode month closes as soon as both totals are entered. All figures use heavy fuel oil only and melted glass (not packed). The page calculates liters per tonne, kilocalories per kilogram melted, and the greenhouse gas rate per tonne melted.
