ISO 45001 §8.1.1 / Saudi MR 3337 — Confined space entry, per L3-SHEE-PR-05-WI-01. Every confined space on site is first identified in the Confined Space Site Register (L4-SHEE-PR-05-R-01) before any entry can be authorised. Every entry is then logged against the Pre-Entry / Atmospheric Test Record (L4-SHEE-PR-05-FRM-01) — the mandatory companion checklist and gas-test record for that entry, kept separate from the general Permit to Work (HSE-FRM-01) since atmospheric testing and the pre-entry checklist items aren't captured on the PTW face. This single page holds both registers, each a separate live Google Sheet tab, same collapsible-section concept as the LOTO Register.
