L3-IMS-800-OC-03
ISO 9001 §8.5.5 / §8.6 — Product shall not be released until all planned arrangements have been satisfactorily completed. Final despatch release requires: AIM inspection pass + pack-to-melt ≥80% + cosmetic grade confirmed + mould number traceability + complete pallet label (6 mandatory fields). Food contact products: no concession without customer written acceptance — zero exceptions. CEO approval for major concessions (>1,000 units or >SAR 50K).
