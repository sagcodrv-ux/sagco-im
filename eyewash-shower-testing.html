🚿 Eyewash & Shower Flush Testing Register

Weekly flush record for every emergency eyewash and shower unit, plus the annual full inspection. Weekly is the general best-practice interval (ANSI Z358.1), not yet confirmed against SAGCO's own legal register (MODON 3.6.1) — confirm the exact figure there and adjust if it differs. A weekly flush is a hands-on check: run the water, look at it, don't just look at the unit.
