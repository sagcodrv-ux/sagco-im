L4-SHEE-PR-03-R-01/02 · ISO 45001 §8.1 — PPE Programme (PR-03) · MHRSD Safety Regulations — tracks every PPE issuance event (who received what, when, how much) and keeps a running stock balance for Safety Shoes, the one PPE line whose quantity is not already managed in the ERP.
SCOPE — general PPE (gloves, ear plugs, hair nets, masks, uniforms, etc.) already has its quantity managed in the ERP Stores system; this register does not duplicate that — it only logs each issuance event, as a durable record of who got what. Safety Shoes are the one exception: because shoes are issued individually and worn out over time, the portal tracks a real running balance here (baseline quantity, issued, remaining) the way general PPE does not need.

Open item for Issam: the Safety Shoes baseline quantities below are read from the ERP's "Count" column as of 18-Oct-2026 (the file's last-saved date) — after every 2026 shoe issuance already in this log. That means Issued/Remaining below only count issuances dated after 18-Oct-2026 going forward; the historical Jan–Sep rows are kept for reference but are not re-subtracted from the baseline. If the ERP Count is actually a start-of-year figure instead, tell me and the math flips.
