L4-SHEE-PR-03-R-01/02 · ISO 45001 §8.1 — PPE Programme (PR-03) · MHRSD Safety Regulations — tracks every PPE issuance event (who received what, when, how much) and keeps a running stock balance for Safety Shoes, the one PPE line whose quantity is not already managed in the ERP.
SCOPE — general PPE (gloves, ear plugs, hair nets, masks, uniforms, etc.) already has its quantity managed in the ERP Stores system; this register does not duplicate that — it only logs each issuance event, as a durable record of who got what. Safety Shoes are the one exception: because shoes are issued individually and worn out over time, the portal tracks a real running balance here (baseline quantity, received, issued, remaining) the way general PPE does not need.
