🚚 Vehicle & Mobile Equipment Pre-Use Inspection Register

Owned by Logistics, not Safety — the driver fills this in every time they take over a vehicle or piece of equipment, not once a day or once a shift. A vehicle running 24 hours with one driver gets one check; a vehicle changing hands twice in a day gets two. A failed item is reviewed and signed off by the Logistics Supervisor and marks the equipment Removed from Service until closed here. Safety can view these records to monitor the programme, but does not fill them in. No legal reference is attached to this register in the CAMP01 roadmap — it is built as good practice, matching the existing forklift and crane checks, kept short on purpose so it stays quick to fill in.
