ISO 9001 §8.4.3 — All incoming materials are subject to verification before use. Certificate of Analysis (CoA) must be received at or before delivery — material placed on HOLD without CoA. If first inspection fails, supplier SCAN is triggered (Sheet 21). MEPS compliance certificate mandatory for all electrical equipment purchases >7.5kW. Fuel oil (HFO) samples retained 90 days from delivery. Governed by OC-03 §D.
