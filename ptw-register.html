ISO 45001 §8.1.1 — The PTW system controls all high-hazard work activities. No high-hazard work shall commence without a valid PTW. The issuing authority must verify that all conditions (isolation, PPE, atmosphere testing, exclusion zones) are in place before signing. PTW invalid if conditions change — must be cancelled and reissued. Governed by OC-01 §A.2–A.9.
