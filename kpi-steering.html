IMS Steering Committee — Strategic KPI Register

Chair: IMS Manager Secretary: IMS Coordinator Meeting: Monthly (implementation) / Quarterly (post-certification)

Scope & Purpose

  • Provide strategic leadership, governance and oversight
  • Approve IMS policies, objectives and targets
  • Review overall IMS performance across all standards
  • Review significant risks and opportunities
  • Review legal and regulatory compliance status
  • Allocate IMS resources and budgets
  • Drive continual improvement and business excellence
  • Review certification readiness — TÜV Austria Stage 2

Applicable Standards

ISO 9001:2015 ISO 14001:2026 ISO 45001:2018 ISO 50001:2018 ISO 37001:2016 ISO 26000 ISO 20400 GRI Standards 2021 EcoVadis

Reporting Flow

Data collection: IMS Coordinator collects and consolidates all Working Group KPI data monthly
Review: IMS Steering Committee reviews consolidated performance quarterly
Escalation triggers: Fatality · Major environmental incident · Legal compliance failure · Confirmed corruption · Major audit NC · High-risk CAPA overdue
Annual output: IMS Management Review report presented to CEO
