OC-01 §A.19
⚠️ Visitor register must be active before Stage 1 audit (25–26 Aug 2026). TÜV Austria auditors will be entered into this register on arrival. All visitors, auditors, customers, contractors, and government officials must be signed in, inducted, and signed out.
ISO 45001:2018 §8.1 — All visitors must receive a site safety induction (emergency exits, assembly point, PPE requirements, restricted areas, prohibited activities) before entering the production area. Escort is required in all hot-end and furnace areas. Badge colour-coding system applies: Green = escorted, Yellow = inducted unescorted, Red = restricted access only.
