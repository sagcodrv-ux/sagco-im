Crane and lifting equipment inspection, per HSE-FRM-18 (Crane Inspection Checklist, Rev.0, effective 2 Nov 2020). Every crane, mobile crane, man lift or crane-with-basket unit is inspected before use against this checklist. When the lift is carried out under a Permit to Work, the Permit ID links this inspection to that permit, the same way Confined Space and LOTO already link to PTW.
