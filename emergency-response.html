ISO 45001 §8.2 / ISO 14001 §8.2 — SAGCO maintains an Emergency Response Plan (EPR) covering all 7 defined emergency scenarios. The EPR is registered with Civil Defense. All personnel must be briefed on muster points, alarms, and emergency contacts during IMS induction. Post-drill debrief is mandatory within 24 hours. Any drill findings requiring corrective action must be entered in Sheet 21 (CAPA Register).
