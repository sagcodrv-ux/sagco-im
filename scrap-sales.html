Scrap Sales — gate-pass level log of scrap material sold off-site (paper/CB trays, mixed scrap, shrink film, wood/plastic pallets), feeding the Waste Management Programme's recycling/disposal-route reporting (cullet recycling and general solid-waste KPIs). Related: Waste Management Register. Monthly Entry Register (below): one row per month — status, line count, totals in MT and PCS. Click a month to open its adjustable entry form, the only place anything is added, fixed or removed; "📊 Report" on each row gives a printable, read-only report of that month's lines (and the Issuance Slip for any one line) — line-level detail is never shown as its own editable table, to avoid a second, redundant register. Customers / Materials: two shared lists (buttons below) that the entry form and reports both read from; anyone with page access can view them, adding or deactivating an entry is limited to Managers and the IMS Coordinator, same as reopening a submitted month.
