ISO 9001 §8.1 / §8.2 / §8.3 / §8.4 / §8.5 / §8.7 — This specification defines quality and customer operational controls from customer requirements review through product release and nonconforming output management. Governing procedure: L2-IMS-800-P-05. KPI #1 (Pack-to-Melt Ratio ≥80%) is the primary quality performance indicator — Sheet 18.
SECTION A — CUSTOMER REQUIREMENTS AND REVIEW
Customer requirements review (6 criteria): (1) Product specification conformity — dimensions, capacity, weight, neck finish, cosmetic grade, and thermal shock; (2) GHG and sustainability data — annual Scope 1+2 data per ISO 14064-1 within 60 days of calendar year end; (3) SGP (Sustainability/Quality questionnaire) — complete within 10 working days of receipt; (4) Complaints — acknowledge within 2 working days; root cause within 10 working days; (5) Regulatory compliance — all applicable Saudi and export destination food contact regulations; (6) Traceability — mould number traceable throughout production and despatch.
SECTION B — PRODUCTION CONTROLS
Pre-shift checklist (5 points — completed by Shift Supervisor before production start): (1) PTW all clear — no live permits conflicting with production; (2) Batch composition verified — batch sheet signed by Batch Master; (3) Equipment status — all production machinery and safety interlocks checked; (4) Quality plan reviewed — current revision of product specification at line; (5) Environmental status — all SEA controls active (bag filters running; secondary containment intact).

SECTION C — PRODUCT IDENTIFICATION AND TRACEABILITY
Mould number traceability: every bottle bears its mould number (engraved/etched) enabling full traceability from mould to finished pallet. Pallet label — 6 mandatory fields: (1) product code; (2) mould numbers on pallet; (3) production date and shift; (4) quantity; (5) QA release status (HOLD / RELEASED / CONCESSION); (6) customer/destination if pre-allocated. Traceability records retained minimum 3 years from despatch date.

SECTION E — NONCONFORMING OUTPUTS (5 Dispositions)
Any non-conforming product must be physically segregated immediately — apply HOLD label (red) and enter in Sheet 21 (CAPA Register). Plant Manager notification within 4 hours for any non-conformance affecting a customer order. Food contact rule: no concession for food contact products without customer written acceptance — no exceptions. CEO approval required for major concessions (>1,000 units or >SAR 50K value).
