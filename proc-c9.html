All IMS monitoring, measurement, internal audit, and management review activities
RevisionRev.02 Pages7 Sections13 Approved ByCEO
This procedure defines the complete performance evaluation framework: KPI monitoring (§9.1), compliance evaluation (§9.1.2), internal audit programme (§9.2), and management review (§9.3). Section 5 contains the 4-layer ESG reporting workflow (IMS Records → 5 Discipline Reports → Integrated ESG Report → Management Review). Section 6 covers 12 ESG data sets mapped to GRI disclosures.
