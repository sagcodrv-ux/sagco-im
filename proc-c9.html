§2Responsibilities 8 Roles

§3.2 — Key Definitions
KPI
A measurable value demonstrating how effectively SAGCO achieves IMS objectives. Leading KPIs are predictive; lagging KPIs are outcome-based.
Compliance Evaluation
A systematic process to evaluate the extent to which SAGCO complies with applicable legal and other requirements.
Internal Audit
A systematic, independent, and documented process for obtaining and evaluating evidence to determine the extent to which audit criteria are fulfilled.
Management Review
A formal top management review ensuring the IMS remains suitable, adequate, effective, and aligned with SAGCO's strategic direction.
ESG Report
The consolidated annual management-level report integrating all five discipline-specific performance review reports, for use in management review, strategic planning, and external disclosure.

§5IMS Performance Review & ESG Reporting Workflow 4-Layer Workflow · Lean Reporting Principle

SAGCO's performance evaluation operates through a four-layer reporting workflow. Existing IMS registers and records are the primary source of all performance data. No additional standalone databases or reporting tools shall be created unless operationally justified. Discipline-specific reports shall be concise (typically 2–4 pages) drawing exclusively from existing IMS registers.

1
IMS Records and Data Sources
Operational records · internal audits · compliance evaluations · incident reports · near-miss logs · objectives tracking · monitoring activities
2
Five Discipline-Specific Review Reports
Quality · Environmental · OH&S · Energy · Sustainability — each produced by the relevant discipline owner (see §6)
3
Integrated ESG Report
Compiled by IMS Coordinator — integrates all five Layer 2 reports · covers all material GRI topics · Scope 1/2/3 GHG · human rights · supplier ESG · overall IMS performance · approved and signed by the CEO
4
Outputs and Actions
Management Review inputs · CAPA Register entries · external GRI/SASB/EcoVadis disclosure · Saudi Vision 2030 reporting obligations

§7KPI Dashboard Monthly RAG · WS18 · L3-910-ML-01

The KPI Dashboard (L3-910-ML-01 / WS18) provides a concise monthly performance snapshot. Updated by respective owners, reviewed by the IMS Coordinator, and published to Senior Management using RAG status. The KPI Dashboard feeds the discipline-specific review reports but is not a substitute for them.

🟢 Green — On Track
Performance meets or exceeds target. Routine monitoring continues.
🟡 Amber — ≤10% Below Target
Corrective action plan required within 14 days. IMS Steering Committee notified.
🔴 Red — >10% Below Target
Steering Committee escalation. CAPA raised per L2-IMS-1000-P-07.
§8Compliance Evaluation WS19 · L4-IMS-912-F-01 · 3 Status Categories

Compliance against all legal and other requirements in the Legal Compliance Register (WS4 / L4-IMS-613-R-01) is evaluated using the Compliance Evaluation Checklist (L4-IMS-912-F-01 / WS19). Results reviewed by the IMS Steering Committee and reported at Management Review. Non-compliances with statutory reporting timeframes notified to the relevant authority within the specified period.

Compliant
All requirements met. No actions required.
Compliant with Minor Observations
Corrective action plan required. Documented and tracked to closure.
Non-Compliant
CAPA raised immediately per L2-IMS-1000-P-07. Regulatory notification within statutory period.

§10Management Review CEO-Chaired · Annual · 12 Required Inputs · WS22 · 5 Days Minutes

The CEO chairs the annual IMS Management Review with the full IMS Steering Committee. The five discipline-specific review reports and the annual ESG Report are the primary structured inputs. Minutes (L4-930-FRM-01) distributed to all Department Heads within 5 working days. Action items tracked in Management Review Register (WS22) until closure.

§12Risks and Opportunities Detailed treatment → L2-IMS-600-P-03

Detailed risk treatment managed through L2-IMS-600-P-03 and the IMS Consolidated Risk Register (WS3).

🔴 Performance Evaluation Risks

  • Discipline-specific reports not completed on schedule, preventing timely ESG Report compilation
  • Layer 1 records incomplete or inaccurate, undermining all downstream reports
  • ESG Report not CEO-approved before Management Review
  • Internal audit programme not covering all four standard clauses within the annual cycle
  • ESG materiality assessment not updated following significant stakeholder or business changes

🟢 Performance Evaluation Opportunities

  • Four-layer reporting workflow providing a clear auditable evidence chain from operational records to external ESG disclosure
  • Annual ESG Report enabling effective EcoVadis assessment responses and customer ESG due diligence
  • Management Review informed by integrated performance evidence across all four standards simultaneously
