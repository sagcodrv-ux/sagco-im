ISO 14001 §8.1 / ISO 50001 §8.1 — This specification defines environmental and energy operational controls for all 9 Significant Environmental Aspects (SEAs) and all 6 Significant Energy Uses (SEUs). Section A covers environmental controls. Section B covers furnace and energy controls. SHC formula: HFO litres × 9,700 ÷ tonnes melted. Governing procedure: L2-IMS-800-P-05.

A.3 — Water Management
Closed-loop cooling water system: target ≥70% reuse of total water input. Metering: SWCC supply meter reading monthly by Water Operator — data entered in Sheet 30 §A by 5th of following month. Leak response: leaks ≥10 L/hr to be reported to Engineering within 24 hours and repaired within 5 working days. Water quality: cooling tower conductivity monitored weekly (target <3,000 µS/cm). Annual target: ≤190,000 m³/yr total consumption by 2027 (OBJ-08).
A.4 — Spill Prevention and Response
All chemical storage areas to have 110% secondary containment (bund capacity ≥ 110% of largest vessel). Spill kits: positioned within 10m of all chemical storage areas; inspected quarterly; replenished within 5 working days of partial use. Spill response: stop source → contain with absorbent → prevent drain entry → notify SHEE Head within 1 hour → assess NCEC 24-hour notification requirement if release leaves site boundary → decontaminate → dispose of contaminated material as hazardous waste → file spill report in Sheet 23 (Incidents).

B.2 — SHC Calculation and Daily Data Collection
SHC (Specific Heat Consumption) formula: SHC = (HFO consumed in litres × 9,700 kcal/L) ÷ tonnes of glass melted. Data collection: HFO volume from Aramco delivery records + daily dip measurement; electricity from SCECO feeder meter readings (6 feeders F9031–F9036); LPG from supplier invoice + flow meter. Daily data entry deadline: all meter readings entered in Energy Planning Register (Sheet 8) within 3 working days. Monthly EnPI report to SHEE Head and Plant Manager by 5th of following month. EMCO validates annual GHG inventory (Scope 1+2) and furnace-level SHC against independent measurement.
