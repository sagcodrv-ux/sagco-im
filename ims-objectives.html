IMS Objectives Register — 2026

ISO §6.2 all standards Owner: IMS Coordinator Review: Annual — Management Review

Purpose

SAGCO establishes measurable objectives at the system level and within each Working Group to drive continual improvement across all four ISO standards. Objectives are reviewed annually at Management Review and progress is monitored quarterly via the IMS Steering Committee.
