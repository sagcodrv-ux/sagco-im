Water Quality Lab Register — the Water half of the planned "Water & Waste Data" scope item; Air Emissions is now live separately. One record per lab test, entered by the Environmental team. The specification shown for every parameter is Kindasa Laboratory's own min/max, exactly as printed on their Potable Water Analysis Customer report. Each row's in/out-of-spec status is computed live from whatever is currently stored — it is never saved as a fixed pass/fail, so correcting a value updates the flag by itself.
Kindasa Spec Thresholds: pH 8.00–8.40  •  Conductivity 350–530 µS/cm  •  TDS 190–295 mg/l  •  Turbidity <1 NTU  •  Residual Chlorine 0.20–0.40 mg/l  •  Total Hardness 10.0–25.0 mg/l  •  Total Iron <0.05 mg/l  •  Nitrate <0.5 mg/l  •  Nitrite <0.05 mg/l  •  Bacteria/Coliform/Fecal Coliform: ND. A reading inside the range but within 20% of either edge (or, for the four ceiling-only parameters, within 20% of the upper limit) shows as a Warning even though it still passes — the same 80% rule used on the Air Emissions page.
