Working Group 3 — People, Ethics & Procurement

Chair / KPI Owner: HR Manager Meeting: Embedded in existing monthly HR meeting No new meeting required

Scope

  • ISO 37001 Anti-Bribery Management System
  • Labour & Human Rights compliance
  • ISO 20400 Sustainable Procurement
  • Competency and training management
  • Ethics and Code of Conduct
  • Whistleblowing and grievance management
  • Supplier ESG screening and evaluation
  • CoI declarations management

Reporting & Monitoring

Monthly: HR Manager monitors grievances; Procurement Manager monitors supplier screening
Annual: CoI declarations, anti-corruption training, whistleblowing test — HR Manager compiles annual evidence package
Monthly: WG3 KPI summary submitted to IMS Coordinator
Escalation: Confirmed corruption case · Human rights violation · Labour law breach · Major supplier ESG failure → immediate escalation to IMS Steering Committee
