L1-IMS-PO-01 — IMS Integrated Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Standards: ISO 9001 · ISO 14001:2026 · ISO 45001 · ISO 50001 · ISO 22000 · ISO 37001

Our Vision

Our vision is to continuously strive to be a trusted and preferred supplier of glass packaging through building strategic partnerships.

Our Mission

To manufacture and deliver high-quality glass packaging solutions that enhance our customers operational performance while consistently meeting international quality, safety, and food-grade standards. We are committed to being a reliable and dependable partner by providing consistent product quality, delivering environmentally responsible and on-time delivery through lean, safe, environmentally responsible manufacturing processes. SAGCO continuously invests in its people, technology, infrastructure, and innovation to strengthen long-term strategic partnerships and support sustainable growth for our customers and stakeholders.

Our Core Values

IntegrityRespectAccountabilityInnovationCustomer Focus

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) is a leading manufacturer of food-grade glass container packaging, operating five HFO-fired furnaces at Jeddah Industrial City Phase 4, Saudi Arabia. SAGCO designs, manufactures, decorates, warehouses, sells and distributes glass containers to customers across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the wider region.

SAGCO is committed to operating an Integrated Management System (IMS) that integrates Quality, Environmental, Occupational Health and Safety, Energy, and Food Safety management in full alignment with ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026, ISO 45001:2018, ISO 50001:2018, ISO 22000:2018, and ISO 37001:2016, and supported by the principles and guidance of ISO 26000, ISO 20400, ISO 37000, and the GRI Standards.

Top management commits to providing the leadership, resources, and governance necessary to achieve this policy and to continually improve the effectiveness of the IMS.

Our Commitments

Quality & Food Safety
Deliver food-grade glass containers that consistently meet customer, regulatory and international quality standards.
Environmental Stewardship
Minimize our environmental impacts, reduce GHG emissions intensity by ≥20% by 2030, and comply with all Saudi environmental regulations.
Safety & Health
Provide a safe and healthy workplace for all employees, contractors and visitors, targeting zero fatalities and a downward LTIFR trend.
Energy Performance
Achieve ≥20% reduction in energy use per unit of production by July 2030 through our structured Energy Management System and ECM programme.
Ethical Conduct
Operate with zero tolerance for bribery and corruption in all forms, maintaining an active Anti-Bribery Management System.
Legal Compliance
Comply with all applicable Saudi laws, regulations, and other requirements including MEWA, NCEC, MHRSD, MODON, and Saudi Labour Law.
People & Human Rights
Promote fair labour practices, prohibit forced and child labour, and respect the human rights of all employees and workers.
Continual Improvement
Systematically monitor, measure, review, and improve our IMS performance through objectives, KPIs, audits, and management review.
Sustainability
Achieve and maintain EcoVadis Silver (≥65) and publish an annual GRI-aligned ESG report, demonstrating our commitment to sustainable business practices.

Governance

This policy is implemented through the SAGCO IMS Governance Framework comprising the IMS Steering Committee and six Subcommittees (Risk & Safety, Environment & Energy, Quality, Procurement, People & Ethics, and Operations). All IMS objectives, targets, and KPIs are reviewed quarterly by the IMS Steering Committee and annually at Management Review.

This policy is communicated to all employees, contractors, suppliers, and interested parties and is made available to the public on request. All employees are expected to understand and contribute to achieving the commitments in this policy.

L1-IMS-PO-02 — Environmental Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Standard: ISO 14001:2026

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) recognizes that the protection of the environment is a fundamental responsibility of our business and a core element of our Integrated Management System. We are committed to minimizing the environmental impacts of our glass manufacturing operations and to continually improving our environmental performance.

Our Environmental Commitments

Legal Compliance
Comply fully with all applicable Saudi environmental laws, regulations and permit conditions including MEWA, NCEC, and MODON requirements.
GHG Reduction
Reduce Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions intensity by ≥20% by July 2030 against the 2026 baseline, derived from our energy reduction commitment.
Waste Management
Maximize waste recycling, minimize hazardous waste generation, and increase cullet utilization to support circular economy objectives.
Water Conservation
Monitor and progressively improve process water recycling rates, installing sub-metering infrastructure to enable accurate measurement.
Pollution Prevention
Prevent pollution through proactive environmental aspect and impact assessments and implementation of operational controls.
Sustainable Procurement
Integrate environmental considerations into procurement decisions and promote responsible sourcing from suppliers who share our environmental values.
Employee Engagement
Train and engage all employees on environmental responsibilities, ensuring environmental considerations are embedded in daily operations.
Continual Improvement
Set, monitor, and review environmental objectives and KPIs through Working Group 2: SHEE, Energy & Environment and the IMS Steering Committee.

Governance

Environmental performance is managed through Working Group 2: SHEE, Energy & Environment, chaired by the Deputy Plant Manager. Environmental KPIs are reported quarterly to the IMS Steering Committee. L1-IMS-PO-03 — Occupational Health & Safety Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Standard: ISO 45001:2018

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) is committed to providing a safe, healthy, and secure working environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors. The prevention of work-related injury and ill health is a non-negotiable priority and a fundamental expression of our respect for the people who work with and for us.

SAGCO operates a glass container manufacturing facility with inherent physical, chemical, thermal, and mechanical hazards. We recognize our responsibility to identify, assess, and control these hazards systematically and proactively.

Our OH&S Commitments

Zero Fatalities
Achieve and maintain zero fatalities across all operations, for all employees and non-employee workers.
Hazard Elimination
Systematically identify, assess, and eliminate or control OH&S hazards through regular HIRA assessments and operational controls.
Legal Compliance
Comply with all applicable Saudi OH&S laws and regulations including MHRSD, MODON, and Saudi Labour Law requirements.
Worker Participation
Consult and involve workers and their representatives in all OH&S decisions, hazard identification, and incident investigation processes.
Incident Prevention
Foster a proactive safety culture through near-miss reporting, safety inspections, and systematic root cause analysis of all incidents.
Emergency Preparedness
Maintain and regularly test emergency response plans for all credible scenarios including furnace incidents, fire, chemical spills, and medical emergencies.
Competence & Training
Ensure all employees and contractors are competent for their roles and receive relevant HSE training, with ≥95% completion of the annual HSE training plan.
Continual Improvement
Monitor OH&S performance through leading and lagging indicators, setting improvement objectives and reviewing performance quarterly.

Governance

OH&S performance is managed through Working Group 2: SHEE, Energy & Environment, chaired by the Operations Manager. OH&S KPIs including LTIFR, LTISR, near-miss reports, and legal compliance are reported quarterly to the IMS Steering Committee. This policy is aligned with ISO 45001:2018 and complies with Saudi regulations M/51, M/36, M/65, and MR 3337.

L1-IMS-PO-04 — Energy Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Standard: ISO 50001:2018Supersedes: L4-EnMS-520-A-3 Rev.3

Policy Statement

At Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO), we are committed to optimizing energy performance across all our operations. This Energy Policy outlines our commitment to responsible energy management to reduce environmental impact, improve operational efficiency, contribute to national and global sustainability goals, and supports SAGCO's Integrated Management System in alignment with ISO 50001:2018.

Primary Energy Objective

Achieve ≥20% reduction in energy use per unit of production by July 31, 2030 (existing organisational commitment).

This target is directly aligned with SAGCO's CO₂ emissions intensity reduction target of ≥20% by July 2030. Based on Q1 2026 Energy Review data (Scope 1: 84.3% of total energy; Scope 2: 15.7%), a 20% uniform energy reduction delivers an equivalent 20% GHG reduction.

Our Energy Commitments

Continual Improvement
Improve energy performance and efficiency through a structured Energy Management System (EnMS) based on ISO 50001:2018.
Legal Compliance
Fulfill all applicable legal and other requirements related to energy use, consumption, and efficiency.
Resource Allocation
Provide the necessary resources to achieve our energy objectives and targets, including competent personnel, training, and financial support.
Sustainable Design & Procurement
Integrate energy performance considerations into the design, procurement of equipment, services, and infrastructure.
Energy Awareness
Promote awareness and involvement of employees and contractors in energy-saving initiatives through training and engagement programs.
Energy Review & Monitoring
Conduct regular energy reviews to identify Significant Energy Uses (SEUs) and prioritize improvement opportunities through data-driven decisions.
Technology & Innovation
Invest in energy-efficient technologies and operational practices to support the transition to a low-carbon economy.
Climate Action
Ensure the Energy Management System not only tracks energy use, but also reflects how energy improvements reduce harmful Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions.

Governance

Energy performance is managed through Working Group 2: SHEE, Energy & Environment, chaired by the Deputy Plant Manager. Energy KPIs including SHC per furnace, EnPI achievement, and ECM programme milestones are reported quarterly to the IMS Steering Committee. We believe that "IT IS EVERYONE'S JOB TO CONSERVE ENERGY."

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) is committed to manufacturing and delivering food-grade glass containers that consistently meet and exceed the quality and food safety requirements of our customers, and comply with all applicable international, national, and regulatory standards. Our Quality & Food Safety Commitments

Customer Focus
Understand and consistently meet customer requirements, targeting a Customer Satisfaction Rating of ≥4.0/5.0 and ≤15 complaints per year.

Food Safety

Traceability
Maintain full product traceability throughout the manufacturing chain, with mock recall capability within 3 hours of initiation.

Supplier Quality

Competence
Ensure all employees are competent, trained, and aware of their contribution to product quality and food safety.
Continual Improvement
Embrace continual learning and best practice adoption in response to technological and market advances; systematically close all nonconformities through CAPA.

Governance

Quality and food safety performance is managed through Working Group 1: Plant & Quality, chaired by the QA Manager. Quality KPIs are reported quarterly to the IMS Steering Committee and annually at Management Review. This policy is communicated to all employees, contractors, and relevant interested parties.

L1-IMS-PO-07 — Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Standard: ISO 37001:2016Supplements: POL-HR-03

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) establishes a zero-tolerance approach to bribery and corruption in all its forms. This policy implements ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System requirements and supplements the SAGCO Anti-Bribery Policy (POL-HR-03). SAGCO is committed to conducting all business with integrity, transparency, and in full compliance with applicable anti-bribery laws and regulations.

Scope

This policy applies to SAGCO, all its employees at all levels and grades, all directors, officers, contractors, consultants, agency workers, and all business associates including suppliers, agents, intermediaries, joint venture partners, and any other third party acting on behalf of SAGCO. This policy explicitly prohibits facilitation payments regardless of whether they are customary or permitted under local law.

Our Anti-Bribery Commitments

Zero Tolerance
Never offer, promise, give, request, agree to receive, or accept any bribe, kickback, or improper payment in any form.
Facilitation Payments
Prohibit all facilitation payments to government officials or any other person, regardless of local custom or practice.
Gifts & Hospitality
Only give or receive gifts, hospitality, or entertainment that are reasonable, proportionate, transparent, and properly recorded.
Due Diligence
Conduct anti-bribery due diligence on business associates who represent SAGCO or with whom significant transactions are conducted.
Conflict of Interest
Require all designated personnel to declare conflicts of interest annually using Form L4-IMS-520-F-01 and manage declared conflicts transparently.
ABMS Controls
Maintain and review the Anti-Bribery Risk Register (BRA-001 to BRA-008) and implement controls proportionate to assessed bribery risks.
Whistleblowing
Operate a confidential whistleblowing channel enabling all persons to report concerns without fear of retaliation, in compliance with POL-HR-04 and ISO 37002.
Training
Deliver mandatory annual anti-corruption and ethics awareness training to all key personnel as defined in the IMS Training Plan.

Governance

Anti-bribery compliance is managed through Working Group 3: People, Ethics & Procurement, chaired by the HR Manager. The ABMS is subject to independent internal audit and management review annually. Any confirmed case of bribery will be investigated and appropriate disciplinary and legal action will be taken in accordance with POL-HR-09 and applicable Saudi law. This policy is aligned with ISO 37001:2016 and Saudi M/25 2024.

L1-IMS-PO-08 — Information Security Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Framework: IT Governance best practice · Saudi PDPL (M/19) · NCA ECC v2Supplements: POL-HR-06

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information assets — including operational data, business records, customer data, employee information, and IMS documentation — from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction. This policy supplements the SAGCO Protection of Company Property Policy (POL-HR-06) and establishes governance for information security as a best practice within the IMS framework.

Our Information Security Commitments

Asset Protection
Protect all SAGCO information assets — physical, digital, and intellectual — from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or theft.
Zero Major Incidents
Target zero major information security incidents through proactive monitoring, access controls, and regular penetration testing.
Data Backup
Maintain 100% automated daily backup success rate with verified integrity and secure offsite copies for all critical business and IMS data.
PDPL Compliance
Comply with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (M/19) in all processing, storage, and transfer of personal data of employees, customers, and interested parties.
Access Control
Implement role-based access controls ensuring information is accessible only to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
Incident Response
Maintain a documented incident response procedure for information security events, including classification, escalation, investigation, and reporting.
Awareness Training
Provide information security awareness training to all employees as part of the annual IMS training programme.
Continual Review
Review information security controls annually and following any significant incident or change in business operations or technology.

Governance

Information security is managed by the IT Manager within Working Group 2: SHEE, Energy & Environment (IT Manager). Information security KPIs are reported quarterly to the IMS Steering Committee. This policy is a governance best practice document — information security is not currently a certification requirement under the four core IMS standards but is included as part of SAGCO's responsible governance framework.

L1-IMS-PO-09 — Human Rights & Labour Practices Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Standards: ISO 26000 §6.3/6.4 · ISO 45001 §5.4 · UN Guiding PrinciplesUmbrella for: POL-HR-01, 02, 05, 07, 08

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) is committed to upholding the human rights and dignity of all persons associated with our operations — including all employees, non-employee workers (contractors), suppliers, and community members. We recognize our responsibility to respect internationally recognized human rights as set out in the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights and to promote fair, safe, and ethical labour practices throughout our value chain.

SAGCO achieved a score of 0 (Green — Fully Compliant) in the Coca-Cola Company SGP full on-site assessment conducted by SGS in December 2025, confirming compliance with the Supplier Guiding Principles on human rights and labour practices.

Our Human Rights & Labour Commitments

Non-Discrimination
Prohibit discrimination in any form based on race, nationality, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other personal characteristic. (POL-HR-01)
Anti-Harassment
Maintain a workplace free from harassment, abuse, or any threatening, intimidating, or exploitative behavior in any form. (POL-HR-02)
Freely Chosen Employment
Ensure all employment is freely chosen. Prohibit all forms of forced, bonded, or compulsory labour. Never withhold workers' personal documents. (POL-HR-08)
Child Labour Prohibition
Employ no person below the legal working age. Implement age verification at recruitment. Maintain a remediation plan for child labour identification. (POL-HR-07)
Fair Working Conditions
Comply with all Saudi Labour Law obligations on wages, working hours, rest periods, and benefits. Provide healthcare to all employees. (POL-HR-05)
Freedom of Association
Respect workers rights to associate freely and to raise grievances without fear of penalty or retaliation, consistent with applicable Saudi law.
Gender Diversity
Promote equal opportunity in recruitment, development, and progression. Implement gender-sensitive practices and report gender diversity metrics in the annual ESG Report.
Supply Chain
Require all suppliers and contractors to comply with this policy, implementing human rights due diligence through the Supplier Code of Conduct and ESG screening process.

Grievance Mechanism

SAGCO operates a formal grievance mechanism accessible to all employees and non-employee workers. All grievances are acknowledged within 3 working days, with ≥80% resolved within 1 week and 100% resolved within 30 days. No person will suffer retaliation for raising a concern in good faith (POL-HR-04). All complaints are handled confidentially.

Governance

Human rights and labour compliance is managed through Working Group 3: People, Ethics & Procurement, chaired by the HR Manager. Labour practices and human rights KPIs are reported quarterly to the IMS Steering Committee. This policy is aligned with ISO 26000 §6.3/6.4, ISO 45001 §5.4, Saudi MR 3337, M/51, and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights. The companion HR Policy Suite (POL-HR-01 through 09) operationalizes the commitments in this policy.

Rev: 2Date: May 2025IMS Clause: ISO 45001 §5.4 · L1-IMS-PO-09

Policy Statement

SAGCO is committed to providing a work environment free from harassment or any behavior that is threatening, abusive, or exploitative — including gestures, language, and physical contact. All employees at all levels are expected and required to abide by this policy. No person will be adversely affected in their employment as a result of bringing complaints of unlawful harassment.

Key Commitments

Zero Tolerance
Any form of harassment, abuse, or intimidation will not be tolerated at any level of the organization.
Reporting & Investigation
Employees must immediately report harassment to their superior or HR. All matters will be investigated and corrective action taken. Handled confidentially.

POL-HR-03

Anti-Bribery Policy

Rev: 2Date: May 2025IMS Clause: ISO 37001 §5.2 · L1-IMS-PO-07

Policy Statement

SAGCO establishes a zero-tolerance approach to bribery and corruption in all its forms. This policy prohibits employees, contractors, and business partners from offering, accepting, or soliciting bribes in exchange for business advantage. SAGCO is committed to maintaining an ethical work environment where decisions are made based on merit, fairness, and adherence to the law.

In case of any reported bribery, an investigation will take place and necessary corrective actions will be taken. All bribery complaints will be handled in a confidential manner.

POL-HR-05

Workplace Policy

Rev: 2Date: May 2025IMS Clause: ISO 45001 §5.3 · Saudi MR 3337 · L1-IMS-PO-09

Policy Statement

SAGCO Workplace Policy outlines the expected behavior, standards, and values that guide how employees interact with each other in the workplace. It creates a framework for maintaining a professional, safe, and respectful environment where employees can perform their best work.

All employees must respect colleagues regardless of role, background, or personal characteristics. Punctuality, quality work, and positive environment are expected.

Rev: 2Date: May 2025IMS Clause: ISO 26000 §6.3 · EcoVadis · L1-IMS-PO-09

Policy Statement

SAGCO is committed to ensuring that no children are employed in its operations. POL-HR-08

Forced Labour Policy

Rev: 2Date: May 2025IMS Clause: ISO 26000 §6.3 · ILO Conventions · L1-IMS-PO-09

Policy Statement

SAGCO does not tolerate any form of forced labour. Employment at SAGCO is freely chosen. Employees have the right to move freely during working hours according to internal rules and to leave the workplace per their employment contracts.

Key Commitments

Document Freedom
SAGCO ensures no employee personal documents (passport, national ID, work permit) are withheld by the company. Employees retain full control of their documents.
No Forced Overtime
Workers will never be forced to work overtime. Any overtime worked is compensated in line with company policy and Saudi labour laws.
Corrective Action
If forced labour practices are identified, the company will investigate immediately and take corrective action.

Policy Statement
