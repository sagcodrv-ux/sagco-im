Visitor & Contractor Management — full digital workflow from invitation to gate clearance. Cleared to Enter (Green) requires: Registration Approved + valid Induction (≤ 12 months) + PPE Issued. Amber = approved & inducted but PPE not yet issued, or induction expiring within 30 days. Red = not approved, never inducted, or induction expired. Safety / Managers create invitations and approve registrations. Security scans at the gate. Safety marks induction. PPE issuer marks PPE Issued.

Scan the visitor’s QR card at the main gate. The system shows registration, induction and PPE status in real time.

After the safety induction session, scan the person’s QR card (or search) and mark Induction Completed.

After issuing the required PPE, scan the person’s QR card (or search) and mark PPE Issued. This is a durable status for the visit.
