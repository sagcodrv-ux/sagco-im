Visitor & Contractor Induction — one QR-coded card per person. Cleared to Enter (green) needs two things together: an induction done within the last 12 months, and today's PPE confirmation — a simple yes/no the security guard ticks by eye for the person's destination, using the printed PPE guide kept in the security room (there's no PPE list on this page — PPE is checked fresh on every visit, it never carries over from a previous day). Amber means partly there (inducted but today's PPE not confirmed yet, or the induction expires within 30 days). Red means not cleared (never inducted, induction expired, or today's PPE was marked No). A returning visitor's own record is simply updated with today's visit — there's no separate visit history. Induction can be confirmed by HSE looking the person up, or by a trainer scanning each attendee's own card at the end of a session. Any safety officer can also scan a card anywhere in the plant for a spot check, which is logged on its own — if PPE is found missing, it can be escalated to the HSE Violation Register with one click.
