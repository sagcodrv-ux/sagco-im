OC-01 §A.10
ISO 45001 §8.1.1 — LOTO must be applied before any work on equipment with hazardous energy. The One Lock Per Person Rule (OLPR) means each worker performing the task applies their own personal lock — no sharing. Equipment cannot be re-energised until every worker has removed their own lock. Governed by OC-01 §A.10.
