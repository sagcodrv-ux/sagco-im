L2-IMS-500-P-02
Leadership and Commitment
ISO 9001:2015 §5 · ISO 14001:2015 §5 · ISO 45001:2018 §5 · ISO 50001:2018 §5
Senior management, IMS Steering Team, Ethics Committee, all SAGCO personnel
RevisionRev.02 Pages6 Sections13 Approved ByCEO
This procedure defines how top management demonstrates leadership and commitment to the IMS, establishes and communicates nine IMS policies, assigns roles and responsibilities, ensures worker participation and consultation, and fulfils human rights and labour obligations. Absorbed former standalone WIs for worker participation (L3-IMS-540-WI-01), communication (L3-IMS-740-WI-01), and speak-up (L3-SUS-PR-09-P-01).
