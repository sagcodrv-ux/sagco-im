Leadership and Commitment

Purpose: This procedure defines requirements for demonstrating leadership and commitment to the IMS, establishing SAGCO's policy framework, assigning organisational roles and responsibilities, ensuring worker participation and consultation, governing communication, the Speak-Up system, and fulfilling the leadership obligations associated with human rights, labour rights, and the Saudisation (NITAQAT) programme. It applies to the CEO, Senior Management, IMS Steering Committee, and all persons under SAGCO's control at Jeddah Industrial City, Phase 4.

§2 Responsibilities 5 Roles
👔 CEO
Approves all nine IMS policies. Chairs Management Review. Reviews KPI Dashboard monthly. Conducts Gemba Walks to operational areas. Personally accountable for SAGCO's human rights commitments and NITAQAT compliance.
🏭 Senior Management
Integrates IMS requirements including human rights and labour obligations into departmental operations. Participates in the IMS Steering Committee. Accountable for IMS performance within their functions.
📊 IMS Manager
(Steering Committee Chair)
Chairs IMS Steering Committee meetings. Oversees IMS coordination and governance. Escalates adverse performance including human rights or labour compliance issues to CEO.
👥 HR Manager
Administers the NITAQAT programme. Manages migrant worker welfare obligations. Maintains workforce diversity records (GRI 405). Coordinates labour compliance monitoring. Reports human rights context changes to IMS Coordinator.
🗂 IMS Coordinator
Maintains all policy documents. Manages worker participation register. Coordinates Speak-Up system. Reports IMS performance to the IMS Steering Committee. Has organisational freedom to report nonconformities to top management without retribution.

§3.2 — Key Definitions
Worker Participation
Involvement of workers in OH&S decision-making — hazard identification, risk assessment, incident investigation, and objectives development.
Worker Consultation
Seeking workers' views before decisions affecting their OH&S, ensuring those views are considered before implementation.
Gemba Walk
Leadership practice in which management visits operational areas to observe conditions, engage workers, and demonstrate visible IMS commitment.
Speak-Up
Confidential channel for workers and interested parties to report concerns on bribery, safety, environmental non-compliance, or labour rights violations without fear of retaliation.
NITAQAT
The Saudi Saudisation programme requiring a defined proportion of the SAGCO workforce to be Saudi nationals, with specific Saudisation targets by category.
Forced Labour
Any work or service extracted from a person under menace of penalty, for which the person has not offered themselves voluntarily, including debt bondage and passport confiscation.

§5 IMS Governance — 3 Working Groups Steering Committee · WG1 · WG2 · WG3

Top management demonstrates leadership and commitment through: CEO signature on all nine IMS policies renewed annually at Management Review; IMS Steering Committee meetings chaired regularly with minutes maintained; monthly KPI Dashboard review with documented CEO sign-off; Gemba Walks to operational areas by the CEO or IMS Manager with records maintained; and direct CEO participation in the annual Management Review.

§8 Human Rights & Labour Obligations UNGPs · L1-IMS-PO-09 · 6 Obligations
SAGCO's leadership is committed to respecting internationally recognised human rights across its workforce and supply chain, consistent with the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, the Human Rights Policy (L1-IMS-PO-09), and the SAGCO HR Policy Suite (CoC + POL-HR-01 through POL-HR-09, CEO-signed May 2025). The Code of Conduct §4.2.11 requires annual CoC awareness training for all employees, recorded in L4-IMS-720-RC-01.
ObligationRequirementEvidence Record
Forced & Bonded Labour ProhibitionNo worker shall be compelled to work through force, threat, or debt bondage. SAGCO shall not withhold wages, retain identity documents, or restrict freedom of movement as a means of coercion.HR records · employment contract review · exit interview records
Child Labour ProhibitionNo person under the age of 18 shall be employed in any role at SAGCO. Age verification is conducted at recruitment using valid government ID.HR recruitment records · age verification log
Migrant Worker WelfareMigrant workers shall receive employment contracts in their native language before departure from their country of origin. Accommodation and welfare facilities shall meet Saudi MO 3337 and applicable bilateral agreements.Contract records · accommodation inspection records
Freedom of AssociationSAGCO respects workers' rights to freedom of association within the Saudi regulatory framework. Worker representatives are elected per HSE Committee procedures and their participation is protected.Worker Participation Register — Workbook Sheet 12
Non-Discrimination & Equal OpportunitySAGCO prohibits discrimination on grounds of nationality, religion, gender, age, disability, or race in recruitment, training, promotion, or terms of employment.HR records · grievance log · diversity data — Workbook Sheet 28
Saudisation (NITAQAT)SAGCO maintains NITAQAT compliance per MHRSD requirements. Saudisation targets by category are monitored monthly by the HR Manager and reported at quarterly IMS Steering Committee meetings.NITAQAT compliance reports · HR workforce data — Workbook Sheet 28
Human rights due diligence is conducted annually as part of the context review (L2-IMS-400-P-01 §7.3) and wherever a significant operational change, new supplier relationship, or labour risk event occurs. Identified risks are captured in the Integrated Risk Register (L4-IMS-610-R-01).

§10 Speak-Up / Whistleblowing System ISO 37001 §8.10 · Royal Decree M/25 2024

SAGCO maintains a confidential Speak-Up channel for all workers, contractors, and interested parties to report concerns on bribery, safety, environmental non-compliance, labour rights violations, or human rights abuses. SAGCO prohibits all retaliation against good-faith reporters under Saudi Royal Decree M/25 (2024).

1
Intake
Concern received, assigned unique reference, acknowledged to reporter where identity known
Within 2 working days
2
Triage
Severity assessed: (A) Immediate risk → CEO & SHEE Head notified immediately; (B) Governance; (C) Operational
Within 3 working days
3
Investigation
Independent investigator appointed. ISO 37001 §8.10 applied for bribery cases. Treated as confidential
Within 30 days
4
Outcome
Findings documented as unsubstantiated or substantiated. Disciplinary action or regulatory notification as required
End of investigation
5
Closure
Speak-Up Log updated, outcome communicated to reporter, anti-retaliation verified
Within 5 days of outcome
Anti-Bribery Governance: Confirmed bribery is reported to NAZAHA and relevant Saudi authorities per Royal Decree No. M/16. Labour rights violations involving criminal conduct are reported to MHRSD within the statutory timeframe. SAGCO maintains a gifts and hospitality threshold register, annual Conflict of Interest declarations from procurement and finance staff, and annual anti-bribery awareness training for all high-risk roles.

§12 Risks and Opportunities Detailed treatment → L2-IMS-600-P-03

Detailed risk treatment is managed through L2-IMS-600-P-03 and the IMS Consolidated Risk Register.

🔴 Leadership Risks

  • Insufficient visible management engagement weakening IMS culture
  • Policy communication failures leaving workers unaware of their obligations
  • Worker participation mechanisms not maintained at required frequency
  • Underdeclared conflicts of interest
  • Human rights violations in the workforce or supply chain not identified or addressed
  • NITAQAT non-compliance triggering MHRSD enforcement action

🟢 Leadership Opportunities

  • Gemba Walk programme accelerating near-miss reporting culture
  • Multilingual worker participation increasing safety improvement suggestions
  • Human rights due diligence improving SAGCO's position in ESG customer assessments and GRI 2021 disclosure
  • Anti-bribery programme supporting ethical procurement relationships
