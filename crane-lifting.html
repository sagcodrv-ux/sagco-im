L4-SHEE-PR-11-R-01 · ISO 45001 §8.1 / SASO — Crane & Lifting Equipment Register, covering the equipment master list (with 3rd-party annual inspection certificate tracking) and the digitised HSE-FRM-18 Operator Pre-Use Inspection Checklist. All cranes, hoists, slings and shackles require a valid annual third-party certificate; SWL marked; pre-use visual inspection before every lift. Governed by OC-01 §A.11.
⚠ The 23-item checklist below reproduces HSE-FRM-18 exactly. The form itself only checks whether a valid 3rd-party certificate exists (item D2) — it doesn't capture the certificate's own expiry date. Since the auto-reminder engine already watches a "Next Inspection Due" field on this register, that expiry is tracked separately per piece of equipment in the master list below, not per checklist entry — add or update it there when a new certificate is issued. The previous placeholder page referenced this register as "Sheet 57"; nav.js and the Operations Hub scopes list it under Sh.45 — flagging this label mismatch rather than resolving it, as it may refer to the master reference workbook's own numbering.
