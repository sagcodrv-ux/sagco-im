Purpose: This procedure defines requirements for IMS planning at SAGCO — risk and opportunity management, hazard identification and risk assessment, environmental aspect evaluation, legal compliance, objectives setting, GHG and Scope 3 emissions planning, energy planning, sustainable procurement planning including third-party due diligence, and management of change. It applies to all planning activities across ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026, ISO 45001:2018, ISO 50001:2018 §6, and ISO 50001:2018 §7.3. All planning outputs feed directly into Clause 8 operational controls.

MoC

Indirect GHG emissions in SAGCO's value chain, both upstream and downstream, as defined by ISO 14064-1 and GHG Protocol.
TPDD
Third-Party Due Diligence — structured screening of suppliers for sanctions exposure, politically exposed persons, adverse media, and ESG risk.

§5Risk & Opportunity Management 5×5 Matrix · TCFD · Quarterly Review

SAGCO uses a 5×5 risk matrix (Likelihood × Severity) to evaluate risks and opportunities. All integrated risks and opportunities are recorded in the IMS Consolidated Risk Register (L4-IMS-610-R-01 / WS3). Risk owners are assigned at Department Head level. Treatment actions and residual ratings updated quarterly and reviewed at Management Review.

Risk Response Levels
1–5
Low
Routine monitoring. Departmental management. No escalation required.
6–11
Medium
Additional controls required. IMS Steering Committee review at next meeting.
12–19
High
Priority action plan. Management Review agenda item. Department Head accountability.
20–25
Critical
Immediate action. CEO notification within 24 hours. CAPA raised per L2-IMS-1000-P-07.
🌡 TCFD Climate Risk Assessment
Physical Risks: Extreme heat events impacting operations · HFO supply disruption · Water scarcity
Transition Risks: Saudi carbon pricing trajectory · NCEC emission limit tightening · Customer ESG procurement requirements
§6Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment ISO 45001 §6.1.2 · 16 Processes · WS13

SAGCO conducts HIRA across all 16 IMS processes annually, following any incident, and after significant change. HIRA covers routine and non-routine activities, emergency situations, and contractor and visitor activities. HIRA results are maintained in the HIRA Register (WS13 / L4-IMS-612-R-01). Worker participation in HIRA reviews is mandatory per ISO 45001 §5.4.

Hierarchy of Controls
1
Elimination — Remove the hazard entirely
2
Substitution — Replace with a less hazardous alternative
3
Engineering Controls — Isolate people from hazard
4
Administrative Controls — Change the way people work
5
PPE — Personal Protective Equipment as last resort

Residual risks rated High or Critical require immediate treatment action. Results feed directly into OC-01 Safety Controls (L2-IMS-800-P-05).

§7Environmental Aspects & SEA ISO 14001:2026 §6.1.2 · WS14

Significant Environmental Aspects are maintained in the SEA Register (WS14 / L4-IMS-612-R-02), evaluated annually using combined criteria of scale · severity · likelihood · regulatory interest · community concern. Environmental aspects from planned changes are assessed through the MoC process before implementation.

§9Legal and Other Requirements 65 Obligations · 7 Regulators · WS4

The Legal Compliance Register (WS4 / L4-IMS-613-R-01) covers 65 legal and other requirements from NCEC, MEWA, MHRSD, Civil Defense, SASO, SEEC, and applicable ISO standards. The IMS Coordinator reviews for new and amended regulations monthly. The register is formally updated quarterly and reviewed at Management Review.

Compliance Status Definitions
Compliant
All requirements met. No actions required.
Compliant with Minor Observations
Department Head corrective action plan within 14 days.
Non-Compliant
CAPA raised immediately per L2-IMS-1000-P-07. Regulatory authority notified within statutory period.
§10Sustainable Procurement & TPDD ISO 20400:2017 · 20% ESG Weighting · WS24 & WS29

All procurement decisions consider ESG criteria in addition to cost, quality, and delivery. The ESG weighting in supplier selection is 20% of total evaluation score. The Procurement Manager maintains the Supplier ESG Register (WS24) covering all Tier-1 and Tier-2 suppliers with annual ESG assessments.

Supplier Risk Categorisation
🔴 High Risk
High-risk geographies, sectors with historical HR issues, or significant environmental impact (furnace equipment, HFO suppliers, primary raw materials). Requires annual ESG assessment + TPDD screening.
🟡 Medium Risk
Moderate ESG exposure. Biennial assessment required.
🟢 Low Risk
Standard commodity and services suppliers. Questionnaire-based self-assessment.
TPDD Screening — High & Medium Risk Suppliers
🌐 Sanctions list screening (UN · EU · OFAC · Saudi) 👤 Politically Exposed Person (PEP) identification 📰 Adverse media screening (HR · corruption · environment) ⚖ Anti-bribery risk assessment

Adverse findings escalated to IMS Coordinator and Legal Representative before contract award. Results recorded in TPDD Log (WS29).

The Supplier Code of Conduct (L1-IMS-PO-08 / L3-SCoC-001) sets minimum requirements on labour rights, environmental management, business ethics, and IMS compliance. Suppliers must sign the SCoC before contract award. Non-compliant suppliers are subject to escalation per L2-IMS-1000-P-07 §9.

§11IMS Objectives, Targets & KPIs ISO §6.2 · SMART · CEO-Approved · WS6

SAGCO maintains IMS objectives (WS6 / L4-IMS-620-F-01) covering quality, environmental (GHG, water, waste), OH&S, energy, and ESG performance. Objectives are SMART and CEO-approved annually at Management Review. Anti-bribery objectives per ISO 37001 §6.2 are integrated covering training completion rates, CoI declaration compliance, Speak-Up case resolution timescales, and gifts threshold compliance.

Monthly RAG Monitoring
🟢 Green — On Track
Performance meets or exceeds target. Routine monitoring continues.
🟡 Amber — ≤10% Below Target
Department Head corrective action plan within 14 days. IMS Steering Committee notified.
🔴 Red — >10% Below Target
IMS Steering Committee escalation. CAPA raised per L2-IMS-1000-P-07.
§12Energy Planning — SEUs & EnPIs ISO 50001 §6.3–§6.6 · 12 SEUs · WS8

The annual energy review covers energy sources and use by type and process; SEU identification covering all five furnaces, compressed air systems, and lighting (12 SEUs total); EnPI calculation and monitoring; energy baseline; and ECM programme status. All outputs maintained in the Energy Planning Register (WS8 / L4-IMS-630-R-01).

Primary EnPI
Specific Heat Consumption (SHC) in kcal per kg packed glass — measured per furnace monthly. Energy baseline established per furnace. EnPI trend chart vs baseline reviewed quarterly.
Data Collection Plan (§6.6)
Parameters · collection method · frequency · responsible owner · accuracy requirements maintained in WS8. All energy data entered within 3 working days of collection period end. Anomalies investigated and documented.

§14Management of Change §6.3 · WS7 · IMS Steering Team Leader Approval

Before implementing any planned change to processes, plant, personnel, or the IMS, a MoC assessment is completed and recorded in the MoC Register (WS7). The MoC identifies risks introduced, required controls, and updates needed to the HIRA, SEA Register, energy plans, legal register, and supplier arrangements. Changes involving energy-using equipment also trigger energy screening per §13. Changes may not proceed without IMS Steering Committee Chair approval.

§16Risks and Opportunities Detailed treatment → L4-IMS-610-R-01

Detailed risk treatment managed through the IMS Consolidated Risk Register (L4-IMS-610-R-01).

🔴 Planning Risks

  • HIRA and SEA registers not updated following process changes
  • Energy baseline data quality issues undermining EnPI validity
  • Legal register updates delayed — non-compliance window
  • MoC not triggered for incremental changes
  • Energy screening bypassed in procurement
  • Scope 3 data quality insufficient for GRI 305-3 disclosure
  • TPDD screening failures exposing SAGCO to sanctions risk or supply chain human rights violations

🟢 Planning Opportunities

  • Systematic energy planning supporting Vision 2030 commitments
  • Integrated risk register enabling cross-functional synergies
  • Proactive legal compliance monitoring
  • ECM programme delivering measurable cost and emissions improvements
  • Sustainable procurement programme strengthening ESG customer credentials
  • Scope 3 accounting enabling science-based target setting
