L2-IMS-600-P-03
Planning and Risk Management
ISO 9001:2015 §6 · ISO 14001:2015 §6 · ISO 45001:2018 §6 · ISO 50001:2018 §6
All IMS risks, opportunities, objectives, legal obligations, energy planning, and change management
RevisionRev.02 Pages7 Sections17 Approved ByCEO
This procedure is the most comprehensive in the IMS — covering HIRA (ISO 45001 §6.1.2), environmental aspects and SEA (ISO 14001 §6.1.2), GHG Scope 1/2/3 inventory (ISO 14064-1), legal compliance (§6.1.3), objectives and KPIs (§6.2), energy planning and SEU identification (ISO 50001 §6.3–§6.6), sustainable procurement and TPDD (ISO 20400), and management of change. All planning outputs feed directly into Clause 8 operational controls.
