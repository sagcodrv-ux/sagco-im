§5 — HIRA Methodology · ISO 45001:2018 §6.1.2 · Hierarchy of Controls
Level 1 — Most Effective
Elimination
Remove the hazard entirely. SAGCO examples: removing redundant chemicals from site; decommissioning obsolete equipment.
Level 2
Substitution / Engineering
Replace or isolate the hazard. Examples: regenerative burner conversion; bag filters; blast shields; enclosed conveyors.
Level 3
Administrative
Change how work is done. Examples: PTW for hot work/confined space; LOTO procedures; work-rest rotation; WBGT monitoring.
Level 4 — Least Effective
PPE
Last resort only. Examples: aluminised heat suit (molten glass); P3 respirators (silica); EN 352 HPE (noise); arc flash Cat.3.
