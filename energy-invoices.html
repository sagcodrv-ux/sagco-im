Energy Invoices — every energy and water invoice in one place: Electricity, LPG, Diesel, Fuel Oil (HFO), Water Supply and Water Discharge, entered by Finance. One record per month, with one tab per type inside it — click a tab to see only that type's invoices, same as the Gate Check-In tabs. Each tab can be filled in two ways: type the month's total quantity and amount directly, or add any number of individual invoice lines (invoice no., date, supplier, quantity, amount — plus account/feeder for Electricity and LPG) and the page sums them automatically. On Electricity and LPG, the Account field autocompletes from account numbers typed on past invoices. Scanned invoices can be attached to each type — more than one per month if there's more than one invoice. The page shows the unit cost for each type (SAR per kWh, per liter, or per m³) and the month's total spend. The month closes once all six types have a total entered.
