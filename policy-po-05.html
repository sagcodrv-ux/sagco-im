Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) is committed to manufacturing and delivering food-grade glass containers that consistently meet and exceed the quality and food safety requirements of our customers, and comply with all applicable international, national, and regulatory standards. The Quality and Food Safety Management System forms an integral part of SAGCO's Integrated Management System (IMS) alongside ISO 14001:2026, ISO 45001:2018, and ISO 50001:2018.

Our Quality & Food Safety Commitments

Customer Focus
Understand and consistently meet customer requirements, targeting a Customer Satisfaction Rating of ≥4.0/5.0 and ≤15 complaints per year.
Product Excellence
Maintain Pack-to-Melt ratio ≥80% and Total Heldware ≤3% through rigorous in-process inspection and quality control systems.
Food Safety
Comply with ISO 22000:2018 as certified under FSSC 22000, ensuring all glass containers are safe for food and beverage contact applications.
Traceability
Maintain full product traceability throughout the manufacturing chain, with mock recall capability within 3 hours of initiation.
Legal Compliance
Comply with all applicable Saudi and international quality and food safety regulations and standards.
Supplier Quality
Require suppliers to meet our quality and food safety standards through the Approved Supplier List and supplier evaluation programme.
Competence
Ensure all employees are competent, trained, and aware of their contribution to product quality and food safety.
Continual Improvement
Embrace continual learning and best practice adoption in response to technological and market advances; systematically close all nonconformities through CAPA.
Document Authorisation
This policy has been reviewed and authorised in accordance with ISO §5.2 document control requirements.
