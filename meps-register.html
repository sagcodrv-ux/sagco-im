ISO 50001 §8.1 / SEEC MEPS Requirements — All new electrical equipment >7.5 kW must meet MEPS efficiency class ≥IE3 (IE4 preferred). MEPS certificate verified at goods receipt before installation authorised. 90-day post-installation performance verification required. Non-compliant equipment: return to supplier. Governed by OC-02 §B.7.
