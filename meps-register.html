MEPS Compliance Register — Minimum Energy Performance Standards (SASO/SEEC): any electrical motor rated from 0.75 kW up to 375 kW must meet a minimum efficiency class of IE3 (IE3 or IE4) before purchase or installation, per SASO. Evidence of this check is required under ISO 50001 §8.3 (Design & Procurement). Pre-Purchase Slip: before buying, log the equipment here — the page checks it against the MEPS range and tells you whether it's accepted to buy; print the slip to attach to the purchase order. A Slip Ref (e.g. MEPS-2026-014) is assigned automatically on save, so the check is on record even if there's no Purchase Reference yet — leave that field blank and add it later once the PO exists. Enclosed Documents (datasheet, efficiency certificate, etc.) can be attached to each equipment line — they belong to that line only, never shared with another line even under the same Purchase Ref. Register: every slip becomes a row here; after delivery, use "Open / Edit" to record whether it was verified at goods receipt. One Purchase Ref can cover several equipment lines — e.g. a single furnace-rebuild contract with many motors — "🖨 Print Slip" prints all lines under one Purchase Ref together.
