ISO 45001 §8.1 / §8.1.1 / §8.2 — This specification defines the detailed execution instructions for all safety and emergency operational controls at SAGCO. It covers Section A (Safety Controls — 17 activity types) and Section B (Emergency Response — 7 scenarios). Governing procedure: L2-IMS-800-P-05. HIRA inputs: Sheet 13.
SECTION A — SAFETY CONTROLS
A.1 — Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
PPE hierarchy: engineering controls first; PPE as last resort per HIRA residual risk. Minimum site PPE: hard hat (EN 397); safety boots (EN ISO 20345:2011); safety glasses (EN 166); hi-vis vest. Hot-end areas: aluminised face shield + aluminised gloves + heat-resistant boots. Batch house: P3 respirator (EN 149:2001+A1:2009) mandatory when transfer points active. PPE inspected monthly; replaced immediately on damage or expiry.

B.3 — Emergency Drill Programme
Minimum 2 drills per year: at least one full-site evacuation drill + one scenario-specific drill (fire, chemical spill, or medical emergency). Drills conducted unannounced (or with minimum 24hr notice). Post-drill debrief mandatory — document: time to muster, headcount accuracy, equipment deployed, observations, lessons learned. Debrief record filed in Sheet 21 (CAPA) if gaps identified. Drill completion tracked via KPI #8 in Sheet 18 (Audit Programme Completion).
