Reporting & Monitoring

Daily: HSSE Representatives collect incident and near-miss data at unit level
Monthly: Operations Manager (OH&S/Risk) + Deputy Plant Manager (ENV/Energy) compile KPI summaries → submit to IMS Coordinator
Quarterly: Legal compliance evaluation completed and submitted to IMS Steering Committee
Escalation: Fatality · Major environmental incident · Legal compliance failure · Negative EnPI trend 2+ consecutive months → immediate escalation to IMS Steering Committee
