§6.5 — Energy Baseline (EnB) Declaration ISO 50001:2018 §6.5 · EnMS Energy Baseline & Savings Study, Sept 2026 (Final)
SAGCO's formally adopted Energy Baseline (EnB) is calendar year 2023, fixed and not modified going forward. HFO is baselined per furnace as Specific Heat Consumption (SHC, kcal/kg melted); electricity, LPG and diesel are baselined facility-wide, pending normalisation variables for a full EnPI model. A documented 3.5%/year equipment-aging degradation rate — sourced from SAGCO's Altec Energy ASHRAE Level 3 Audit financial model, not independently derived — is applied to the HFO baseline only, compounding monthly, to produce a forward-looking Degradation-Adjusted Expected SHC used to evaluate performance (§9.1). Four months of Furnace 3 data (Jan/Feb/Jul/Aug 2023) are excluded from its baseline calculation due to abnormally low draw (below 60% of normal production).

HFO Energy & Financial Savings — January 2024 to August 2026 (vs. Degradation-Adjusted Expected SHC)
Facility-wide result
HFO consumption came in 4.17% below the degradation-adjusted expectation across the facility — SAR 2,620,309 in calculated savings over the study period, against the 3.5%/year benchmark. The three non-rebuilt furnaces (F3–F5) show a stronger reduction on their own (5.34%) than the facility-wide figure; the two rebuilt furnaces (F1, F2) are roughly flat overall (−0.81%) — the positive result is driven primarily by the non-rebuilt furnaces, not the rebuilds. F1's improvement coincides temporally with its Nov 2025 rebuild but the rebuild effect is not isolated from other variables; F2's rebuild is still under completion and not yet reflected.
