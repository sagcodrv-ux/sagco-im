IMS Scope Statement — ISO §4.3

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) has established, implemented, maintained and is continually improving an Integrated Management System (IMS) covering Occupational Health & Safety (ISO 45001:2018), Environmental Management (ISO 14001:2015), and Energy Management (ISO 50001:2018).

The IMS applies to all glass container manufacturing operations at SAGCO's site at Jeddah Industrial City Phase 4, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, encompassing all production furnaces (F1–F5), forming, annealing, inspection, packaging, utilities, maintenance, procurement and supporting management functions within the defined site boundary.

ISO 9001:2015 is monitored within this framework. ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management is integrated as a reference framework only and is not in certification scope. TÜV Austria is the appointed certification body for ISO 45001, ISO 14001 and ISO 50001.
