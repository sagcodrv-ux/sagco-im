Hot work checklist, per the SAGCO Hot Work Checklist (Rev.00, 24 Nov 2025) and OSHA 29 CFR 1910.252. Filled once per job, at the point the Permit to Work for hot work is released — covers the Pre Hot Work, Execution, Post Hot Work and Permit Closure stages on a single record. The Permit ID links this checklist to its PTW, the same way Confined Space and LOTO already link to PTW.
