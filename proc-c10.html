§3.2 — Key Definitions
Nonconformity (NCR)
Non-fulfillment of a requirement — IMS requirements, ISO standard clauses, legal obligations, or customer requirements.
Corrective Action
Action to eliminate the root cause of a nonconformity and prevent recurrence.
Near-Miss
An incident where no injury or damage occurred but which had the potential to do so. Investigated with the same rigour as reportable incidents.
Continual Improvement
Recurring activity to enhance performance through the PDCA cycle, resulting in measurable improvement in IMS objectives over time.
SCAN
Supplier Corrective Action Notice — formal written notification requiring a supplier to investigate and correct a defined nonconformity against SAGCO's requirements or the Supplier Code of Conduct.
Human Rights Grievance
A concern alleging a violation of or risk to human rights, including forced labour, discrimination, unsafe conditions, or denial of freedom of association.

7-Step CAPA Workflow
1
Raise NCR & Assign Owner
Record in CAPA Register (L4-1000-RC-01 / WS21). Assign owner and classify severity.
2
Containment
Immediate action to prevent harm, environmental release, or product escape.
3
Root Cause Analysis
5-Why as standard method · Ishikawa (fishbone) for fatal or critical-injury incidents. Both immediate causes and underlying management system contributors must be identified.
4
Corrective Action Plan
Define actions, owners, and due dates. Plans for Critical and Major CAPAs require IMS Manager approval before implementation.
5
Implementation
Actions executed and evidenced. Progress tracked in CAPA Register.
6
Effectiveness Review
IMS Coordinator verifies closure evidence. Recurrence within 12 months triggers re-opening.
7
Close
CEO or IMS Manager sign-off for Critical and Major CAPAs. IMS Coordinator sign-off for Minor.
§6Incident Investigation ISO 45001 §10.2 · 24h Reportable · 5 Days Near-Miss · Statutory Notification

All TRIR-reportable incidents, near-misses, dangerous occurrences, and occupational diseases must be investigated. The lead investigator is appointed by the SHEE Head and must be independent of the event.

🚨 Reportable Incidents
Investigation commences within 24 hours. Statutory notifications to MHRSD and/or Civil Defense within the timeframe specified in Saudi Labour Law M/51.
⚠️ Near-Misses
Investigation commences within 5 working days. Investigated with the same rigour as reportable incidents. Lessons learned presented at the next Toolbox Talk.
🔍 Root Cause Analysis Methods
5-Why: Near-misses and minor incidents.
Ishikawa (fishbone): Major incidents and fatalities — identifies both immediate causes and underlying management system contributors.
👷 Worker Participation
Worker participation in incident investigations is mandatory per ISO 45001 §10.2(b). No worker shall be penalised for reporting an incident or near-miss in good faith.

§8Supplier Corrective Action & Escalation 3-Tier Escalation · SCAN → Improvement Plan → Suspension

When a supplier nonconformity is identified — through incoming goods inspection, audit finding, customer complaint, or ESG assessment — the Procurement Manager issues a SCAN to the supplier within 5 working days. The SCAN specifies the nonconformity, the SCoC requirement breached, the required RCA method, and the response deadline.

SCAN Response Deadlines: Critical — 14 days · Major — 30 days · Minor — 60 days
T1
Trigger: First occurrence of nonconformity or ESG assessment finding
Issue SCAN. Supplier submits RCA and corrective action plan within stated timeframe. Procurement Manager reviews and approves plan.
Procurement Manager
T2
Trigger: Repeated nonconformity (same issue within 12 months), SCAN not resolved, or TPDD adverse finding
Issue formal Supplier Improvement Plan. Escalate to IMS Steering Committee. Increase monitoring frequency. Suspend new POs until plan approved and initial actions verified.
IMS Steering Committee Chair
T3
Trigger: Persistent non-compliance after Tier 2 · confirmed human rights violation · confirmed bribery · imminent risk to product safety or legal compliance
Suspend or terminate supplier relationship. Notify alternative sourcing. Document decision. Update Supplier ESG Register. Notify relevant authority where required by law.
CEO
§9Human Rights Grievance Management GRI 2-25 · Critical Priority · 5-Step Process · Anti-Retaliation

Human rights grievances — including concerns about forced labour, child labour, discrimination, unsafe conditions, migrant worker welfare, or freedom of association — are received through the Speak-Up channel, direct HR report, or HSE Committee. All human rights grievances are treated as Critical-priority matters. SAGCO prohibits retaliation against any person raising a grievance in good faith.

1
Intake
Within 2 working days
Acknowledged to reporter where identity known
2
Triage
Within 3 working days
Assessed for immediate safety risk, legal exposure, and severity
3
Investigation
Independent party
HR Manager, IMS Coordinator, or external investigator
4
Outcome
Remediation
Remediation actions implemented. MHRSD or relevant authority notified where required.
5
Closure
Anti-retaliation verified
Outcome communicated to grievant. CAPA raised if system improvement needed.

Anonymised summaries reported at Management Review and disclosed in the annual ESG Report under GRI 2-25.

§10Bribery Investigation ISO 37001 §8.10 · NAZAHA Notification · Royal Decree M/16
When a bribery concern is received, SAGCO conducts a formal investigation per ISO 37001 §8.10. The lead investigator must be independent of the matter. The investigation is confidential throughout. Confirmed bribery is reported to NAZAHA and the relevant Saudi authority per Royal Decree No. M/16. Anti-retaliation is verified at closure. Where system improvement is identified, a CAPA is raised per §5. Outcomes recorded in the Speak-Up Case Log and reported at Management Review.
§11Continual Improvement PDCA · 8 Improvement Sources · Annual Baseline Measurement

Improvements are assigned as actions in the CAPA Register (L4-1000-RC-01) or the Objectives Register (WS6). IMS performance improvement is measured annually against the previous year's baseline for each IMS objective.

§13Risks and Opportunities Detailed treatment → L2-IMS-600-P-03

Detailed risk treatment managed through L2-IMS-600-P-03 and the IMS Consolidated Risk Register (WS3).

🔴 Improvement Risks

  • CAPAs not closed within agreed timescales
  • Root cause analysis addressing symptoms rather than underlying causes
  • Near-miss underreporting masking the actual hazard profile
  • Supplier nonconformities escalating undetected
  • Human rights grievances not investigated with appropriate independence or urgency
  • Energy performance CAPAs not progressing within required timescales

🟢 Improvement Opportunities

  • Well-evidenced multi-standard CAPA system demonstrating operational discipline to TÜV Austria
  • Near-miss programme building a proactive safety culture
  • Supplier corrective action process strengthening supply chain ESG performance and SAGCO's GRI 414 disclosure
  • Human rights grievance mechanism demonstrating responsible business conduct
  • Energy performance improvement through ECM programme delivering GHG intensity reduction
