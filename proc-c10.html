L2-IMS-1000-P-07
Improvement and Corrective Action
ISO 9001:2015 §10 · ISO 14001:2015 §10 · ISO 45001:2018 §10 · ISO 50001:2018 §10
All nonconformities, incidents, improvement opportunities, and corrective actions across the IMS
RevisionRev.02 Pages6 Sections15 Approved ByCEO
This procedure is the most comprehensive improvement framework in the IMS. It covers the complete 8-step CAPA process, incident investigation with statutory notification, near-miss programme, 3-tier supplier corrective action (SCAN), human rights grievance management (GRI 2-25), bribery investigation process, and continual improvement from 8 defined streams. All former standalone WIs absorbed — no L3 work instructions required for Clause 10.
