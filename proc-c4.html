All SAGCO activities within the IMS scope boundary — Jeddah Industrial City Phase 4
RevisionRev.02 Pages5 Sections12 Approved ByCEO
This procedure defines how SAGCO determines its external and internal context, identifies interested parties, defines the IMS scope, and maps all IMS processes. All step-by-step instructions are fully incorporated — no additional L3 work instructions are required for Clause 4. The procedure absorbed all former standalone work instructions for PESTLE/SWOT review, interested parties assessment, scope control, and process mapping.
