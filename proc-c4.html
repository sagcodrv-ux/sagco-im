Purpose: This procedure defines how SAGCO establishes, maintains, and reviews its understanding of the organisational context. It covers the strategic foundation, external and internal issues, interested party requirements, IMS scope, and business process architecture. It applies to all functions within the IMS scope at Jeddah Industrial City Phase 4 under all four ISO management systems. All step-by-step instructions are fully incorporated — no additional L3 work instructions are required for Clause 4.

§5 Strategic & Cultural Foundation Vision · Mission · Values · BPM

SAGCO's Vision, Mission, and Values are the strategic and cultural foundation of the IMS — formally defined, maintained, and CEO-approved in the L1 IMS Framework Manual (the single controlled source). The IMS follows a PDCA cascade: strategic foundation → context analysis → risk assessment → objectives & targets → operational controls → monitoring & review → continual improvement.

🎯 Vision
To continuously strive to be a trusted and preferred supplier of glass packaging through building strategic partnerships, harnessing talented people, and committing to sustainability.
🏭 Mission
To manufacture and deliver high-quality glass packaging solutions that enhance customers' operational performance while consistently meeting international quality, safety, and food-grade standards through lean, safe, and environmentally responsible processes.
⭐ Core Values
Integrity Respect Accountability Innovation Customer Focus
Source: HR-signed Code of Conduct, CoC §3, May 2025

§6 External & Internal Context ISO §4.1 · PESTLE · SWOT

SAGCO reviews its organisational context at least once per year and whenever a significant trigger event occurs. The review follows a structured sequence: Vision, Mission, and Values define strategic intent → PESTLE identifies external issues → internal context inputs (KPIs, audit results, Management Review outputs) identify internal issues → combined outputs feed risk assessment and objective planning in L2-IMS-600-P-03.

§8 IMS Scope ISO §4.3 · CEO-Signed · No Exclusions

The IMS covers glass container and bottle manufacturing at Jeddah Industrial City, Phase 4 — including all furnaces, forming, annealing, inspection, packaging, warehouse operations, and all associated management and support processes — under ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026, ISO 45001:2018, and ISO 50001:2018. The certified QMS (ISO 9001:2015) and Food Safety MS (ISO 22000:2018) are integrated within this scope. No exclusions apply.

The authoritative IMS Scope Statement is maintained in the L1 IMS Framework Manual. Any proposed change to scope requires a Management of Change (MoC) assessment per L2-IMS-600-P-03 before CEO approval.

§10 Risks and Opportunities Detailed treatment → L2-IMS-600-P-03

Detailed risk treatment is managed through L2-IMS-600-P-03 and the IMS Consolidated Risk Register (Workbook Sheet 3). Key context-specific risks and opportunities are noted below.

🔴 Risks

  • Incomplete PESTLE or SWOT analysis leading to unidentified strategic risks
  • Failure to update context register following significant trigger events
  • Regulatory changes not captured before their effective date
  • Human rights issues in workforce or supply chain not identified in time
  • NITAQAT non-compliance resulting in operational restrictions
  • Strategic objectives not translated into measurable IMS objectives

🟢 Opportunities

  • Proactive context analysis identifying early signals of regulatory or market change
  • Stakeholder engagement revealing customer ESG and sustainability requirements
  • Alignment with Saudi Vision 2030 providing competitive differentiation
  • Human rights due diligence improving SAGCO's position in customer ESG assessments
  • Rigorous context traceability demonstrating strategic planning maturity to TÜV Austria
