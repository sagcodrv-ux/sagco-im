L4-SHEE-PR-XX-RC-02 · ISO 45001 §8.1/§6.1 · GHS (UN Purple Book) / SASO GHS · Saudi Labour Law · EcoVadis Environment & Labour — identifies and classifies every chemical/hazardous material held in Stores, tracks whether each has a current GHS-compliant SDS on file, and provides the durable record an auditor can walk through store-by-store.
SCOPE — this register covers exactly the 40 items on Issam's own OL_100126 Stores stock sheet (34 oils/greases/lubricants + 5 hot/cold-end glass coatings + 1 unclassified item), nothing more. Chemicals for which an MSDS exists but which are not on that stock sheet are intentionally not included here.
⚠ Source: OL_100126.xlsx (Issam, Oct 2026) for every row's Item Code/Description/Supplier/UOM/Location/Stock Qty. GHS classification data for the 6 items that have a supplier SDS is seeded from those SDS documents as supplied — 2 of the 6 are pre-GHS legacy documents with no signal word or pictogram (flagged below). The remaining 34 items have no SDS on file at all and are flagged "SDS Pending" until one is requested from the supplier and uploaded here.
