ISO 45001 §8.1 — Radiography (NDT using X-ray or gamma sources) requires a dedicated permit log separate from the general PTW register because it involves radiation sources and has specific regulatory requirements. Radiation Safety Officer (RSO) must verify exclusion zone and dosimetry before work commences. Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Magnetic Particle (MT), and Dye Penetrant (PT) tests are lower risk but still require PTW.
