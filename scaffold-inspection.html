ISO 45001 §8.1 / OC-01 §A.9 — Every scaffold must be inspected before first use, after any alteration, after adverse weather, and at weekly intervals while in use, per L3-SHEE-PR-09-WI-01 and the bilingual HSE-FRM-09 Scaffold Inspection Checklist. Each inspection is logged below against the Scaffold Inspection Register (L4-SHEE-PR-09-R-01). GREEN tag = SAFE TO USE. RED tag = UNSAFE — do not use; withdraw from service until re-inspected. Only a competent scaffold inspector may change tag status.
