SAGCO IMS Governance Framework

Purpose

To establish an effective governance framework for the development, implementation, certification, maintenance and continual improvement of the SAGCO Integrated Management System aligned with ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026, ISO 45001:2018, ISO 50001:2018, ISO 22000:2018, and ISO 37001:2016.

Core Standards
