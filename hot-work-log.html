OC-01 §A.7
⚠️ Hot work log must be populated before Stage 2 audit. Reference each entry to its PTW number. 30-minute post-work fire watch completion is mandatory under HSE-FRM-01 and must be recorded for every hot work activity.
ISO 45001:2018 §8.1 — Hot work (welding, cutting, grinding, open flame) requires a valid PTW (HSE-FRM-01), a fire watch during work and for 30 minutes after, and a fire extinguisher present at point of work. This log records execution and post-work fire watch completion, complementing the PTW register.
