⚙ Harness & Fall Protection Equipment Inspection Register

Per-item inspection record for harnesses, lanyards, lifelines and anchor points — pre-use and the 6-monthly formal check. The Working at Height register tracks that an inspection happened on a given date; this register is the inspection itself, item by item. Any equipment that fails is marked Removed from Service and must not be reissued until closed here.
