Purpose: This procedure defines requirements for managing SAGCO's IMS support processes — resources, competence, awareness, communication, and documented information control. It applies to all activities, processes, and personnel within the IMS scope at Jeddah Industrial City, Phase 4, covering ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026, ISO 45001:2018, and ISO 50001:2018 §7. Clauses addressed: Resources (7.1) · Competence (7.2) · Awareness (7.3) · Communication (7.4) · Documented Information (7.5).

Overall accountability for adequate IMS resources. Approves all L1 and L2 documents.

§3.2 — Key Definitions
Competence
The ability to apply knowledge and skills to achieve intended results, demonstrated through education, training, experience, or evaluation.
Organisational Knowledge
Knowledge specific to SAGCO derived from experience — furnace operating parameters, ECM outcomes, and lessons learned from investigations and audits.
Maintained Document
Documented information kept up to date — procedures, policies, forms.
Retained Record
Documented information providing evidence of results achieved — training records, calibration certificates, audit reports.

§7IMS Awareness 8 Awareness Activities · English · Arabic · Urdu · L4-IMS-920-A-01

All persons under SAGCO's control must be aware of: the IMS policies; their contribution to IMS effectiveness; the benefits of improved performance; and the consequences of nonconformity. The IMS Events Calendar (L4-IMS-920-A-01) governs the schedule, audience, frequency, language, and records for all awareness activities.

  • 🆕
    IMS Induction — All new employees and contractors before commencing work
  • 📋
    Annual Policy Briefing — All personnel · policy acknowledgement signed annually
  • 🔨
    Weekly Toolbox Talks (TBT) — Production, maintenance, and contractor teams · safety, environmental, and quality topics
  • ⚡
    Monthly Energy Performance Updates — Production personnel · SHC trends and ECM programme status
  • 🌿
    Quarterly Environmental Awareness — All departments · waste segregation, spill response, permit conditions
  • ⚖️
    Annual Anti-Bribery Awareness — Per ISO 37001 §7.3 · high-risk roles · gifts register, CoI, Speak-Up
  • 🚨
    Emergency Drill Debriefs — All participants · lessons learned and corrective actions
  • 🔄
    Role-Change Briefings — Within 30 days of any role change · updated responsibilities and relevant IMS requirements
🌐 All safety-critical communications delivered in English, Arabic, and Urdu

§11Risks and Opportunities Detailed treatment → L2-IMS-600-P-03

Detailed risk treatment managed through L2-IMS-600-P-03 and the IMS Consolidated Risk Register (L4-IMS-610-R-01).

🔴 Support Risks

  • Loss of key knowledge holders without succession planning
  • Safety-critical tasks performed without verified L7 certification
  • Calibration failures undermining energy and quality data reliability
  • Use of superseded documents at point of work
  • Communication barriers for multilingual workers reducing safety reporting rates

🟢 Support Opportunities

  • Structured organisational knowledge management capturing furnace optimisation and ECM outcomes
  • Multilingual awareness communications improving near-miss reporting rates
  • Digitalised document control providing real-time access and supporting remote audit readiness
  • Building an internal auditor pool through the IMS training programme
