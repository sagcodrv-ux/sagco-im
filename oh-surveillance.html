OC-01 §A.14
ISO 45001 §8.1 / Saudi Labour Law M/51 — Occupational health surveillance mandatory for all workers exposed to noise, silica dust, heat, physical demand, or confined space work. Results confidential. Workers found unfit for specific task must be redeployed — not dismissed. Pre-employment baseline required before starting in hazardous roles.
