L4-SHEE-PR-XX-RC-01 · ISO 45001 §8.1/§9.1 · Saudi Labour Law M/51 · SMETA (ETI 3) · EcoVadis Labour & Human Rights — plans and evidences occupational health surveillance for exposed workers, and provides the durable record that closes CAPA-2026-003. OH Surveillance Programme defines who is tested for what and how often; Surveillance Log is one row per round per programme line.
PRIVACY BY DESIGN — this register holds COUNTS, DATES and FITNESS-OUTCOME TOTALS per work area only. It has no field for names, employee IDs, Iqama numbers, or individual medical results — neither table below is capable of holding one. Individual medical files stay confidential with the occupational physician/OH clinic provider (Saudi PDPL — health data is sensitive). This is what makes both tabs shareable directly with TÜV, EcoVadis and SMETA auditors with no redaction step.
⚠ Source: Issam's own Rev 01 reference workbook (Oct 2026), built from one worker's Ibn Sina College Hospital file (Feb & Jul 2025). The 8 Programme lines and 3 draft Log rounds below are seeded from that file exactly as supplied — frequencies are proposals pending occupational-physician sign-off, and the 3 draft rounds still need real Workers Required/Examined counts from HR (currently blank, same gap the source file flagged). Document code L4-SHEE-PR-XX-RC-01 is also still a placeholder pending Issam's assignment.
