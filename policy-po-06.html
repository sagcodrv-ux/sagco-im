L1-IMS-PO-06 — Sustainability Policy

Rev: 01Date: June 2026Standards: ISO 26000 · ISO 20400 · GRI Standards 2021 · EcoVadis

Policy Statement

Saudi Arabian Glass Company Ltd. (SAGCO) recognizes that sustainable business practices are essential to our long-term success and to the well-being of the communities, environment, and societies in which we operate. We are committed to creating long-term economic, environmental, and social value for all our stakeholders, and to operating our business in a manner that is responsible, transparent, and aligned with global sustainability frameworks.

SAGCO targets EcoVadis Silver (≥65) and publishes an annual GRI Standards-aligned ESG Report as external evidence of our sustainability performance and governance.

Our Sustainability Commitments

ESG Reporting
Publish an annual ESG Report by end of July each year, aligned with GRI Standards 2021, and submit to EcoVadis annually targeting Silver (≥65).
Circular Economy
Increase cullet utilization rates progressively, promote glass recycling, and minimize waste to landfill in support of circular economy principles.
Climate Action
Reduce GHG emissions intensity by ≥20% by July 2030 and monitor and disclose Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions in annual ESG reporting.
Social Responsibility
Promote fair labour practices, community engagement, and respect for human rights across our operations and supply chain.
Sustainable Procurement
Integrate sustainability criteria into all significant procurement decisions in alignment with ISO 20400 and EcoVadis Sustainable Procurement theme.
Stakeholder Engagement
Engage transparently with customers, employees, suppliers, regulators, and communities on our sustainability performance and commitments.
Third-Party Assurance
Maintain SGS Social Responsibility audit compliance (Green rating) and TÜV Austria IMS certification as independent validation of our commitments.
Continual Improvement
Set, monitor, and improve ESG objectives and targets annually, using EcoVadis assessment results and Management Review outputs to drive improvement.

Governance

Sustainability performance is owned by the IMS Manager and coordinated by the IMS Coordinator. ESG data is collected quarterly from all functional owners and consolidated for the IMS Steering Committee review. This policy is aligned with ISO 26000, ISO 20400, and the GRI Standards 2021 framework.
