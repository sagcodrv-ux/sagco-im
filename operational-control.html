§5 — High-level overview
ISO §8.1 — This procedure governs the what, who, and when of all SAGCO operational controls. The how is detailed in the three L3 Operational Control Specifications. All planning outputs from Clause 6 (HIRA, SEA, Risk Register, Objectives, Legal Compliance, Energy Planning) feed directly into the operational controls documented here and in the OC specifications.

§8 — MANAGEMENT OF CHANGE AT OPERATIONAL LEVEL
MoC triggers: new equipment; process parameter change; new chemical introduction; furnace modification; production line reconfiguration; new contractor with different scope; regulatory change affecting operations. Before implementation: HIRA update required (Sheet 13); SEA re-evaluation if environmental aspect affected (Sheet 14); Energy Review update if SEU affected (Sheet 8); Risk Register update (Sheet 3). All changes entered in MOC Register (Sheet 7) with SHEE Head and Plant Manager approval. CEO approval required for major MoC events.
