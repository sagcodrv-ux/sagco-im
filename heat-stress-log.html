OC-01 §A.13
⚠️ Jeddah Summer (June–September): WBGT monitoring mandatory twice daily (06:00 and 12:00). If ambient temperature exceeds 30°C at 09:00, notify SHEE Head immediately. Work suspended at WBGT >35°C — Plant Manager + SHEE Head joint decision required.
ISO 45001 §8.1.1 / Saudi MO 3337 — WBGT measures combined effect of temperature, humidity, wind, and radiant heat. Heat stroke is a medical emergency — call 911 immediately. All workers in Yellow zone and above must be briefed on heat symptoms: heavy sweating, dizziness, rapid pulse, nausea, confusion.
