L2-IMS-800-P-05
Operational Control
ISO 9001:2015 §8 · ISO 14001:2015 §8 · ISO 45001:2018 §8 · ISO 50001:2018 §8
All SAGCO operational processes, contractors, and externally provided services within IMS scope
RevisionRev.01 Pages9 Sections13 Approved ByCEO
This procedure governs the what, who, and when of all operational controls. The how is detailed in three L3 Operational Control Specifications. All Clause 6 planning outputs feed directly into these controls. Contains an embedded high-level process overview flowchart (§5).
