OC Specs

Purpose: This procedure defines the operational control framework for SAGCO's IMS. It governs how SAGCO plans, implements, and maintains controls for all processes needed to deliver conforming products and services while meeting ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2026, ISO 45001:2018, and ISO 50001:2018 §8. It is the governing document for three subordinate Operational Control Specifications (OC-01, OC-02, OC-03) that contain detailed execution requirements. Together, these four documents constitute the complete Clause 8 documentation for SAGCO's IMS.

§2Responsibilities 8 Roles
👔 CEO
Accountable for the overall operational control framework. Approves significant operational changes affecting IMS scope or certification boundaries.
🏭 Plant Manager
Overall responsibility for operational control implementation across all production areas. Approves operational MoC assessments. Ensures controls are resourced and maintained.
⚠️ SHEE Head
Document owner for OC-01 (Safety and Emergency Controls). Leads the emergency drill programme. Approves PTWs for high-risk activities. Coordinates regulatory notifications.
⚡ Energy Coordinator
Document owner for the energy controls section of OC-02. Monitors SEU operating criteria. Coordinates furnace operating parameters with shift supervisors.
📋 QA Manager
Document owner for OC-03 (Quality and Customer Controls). Approves all product release decisions. Manages nonconforming output disposition.
🏭 Procurement Manager
Owns the external provider controls section of OC-03. Manages supplier operational compliance. Issues SCANs for supplier nonconformities.
👷 Shift Supervisors
Implement operational controls within their areas. Issue PTWs for routine activities. Report deviations and near-misses. Maintain operational records.
🗂 IMS Coordinator
Maintains this procedure and the three OC specifications. Coordinates MoC reviews. Tracks operational CAPA actions. Prepares operational performance data.

§3.2 — Key Definitions
Operational Control
A measure applied to a process or equipment to ensure it operates within defined criteria for safety, environment, quality, and energy performance.
Permit to Work (PTW)
A formal written authorisation defining scope, conditions, and controls required before a non-routine or high-risk activity may start.
Significant Energy Use (SEU)
An energy use accounting for substantial consumption or offering considerable improvement potential. SAGCO has 12 SEUs: all five furnaces, compressed air systems, and lighting.
Management of Change (MoC)
A controlled process for evaluating and managing risks introduced by any planned change before implementation.
Nonconforming Output
A product or material that does not meet specified requirements and must be identified, controlled, and dispositioned before release or use.

§7Contractor and Visitor Management 7 Pre-Mobilisation Requirements · Visitor Register L4-IMS-800-RC-01
Contractor Pre-Mobilisation Checklist — 7 Requirements
  • Valid SAGCO site safety induction completed within the previous 12 months
  • Relevant trade licences and certifications current
  • Contractor company safety record reviewed — TRIR data requested
  • SAGCO-specific risk assessment reviewed and acknowledged
  • PPE compliant with SAGCO minimum standards
  • Emergency evacuation routes and assembly points briefed
  • ESG and SCoC requirements acknowledged (supply contractors)
During on-site work: Contractors are subject to all SAGCO safety controls in OC-01, including the PTW system, LOTO requirements, and area-specific controls. Contractor activities are included in HIRA reviews. Safety performance reported to SHEE Head monthly.
Visitors: Must be registered, escorted, and briefed on safety rules and evacuation procedures. Minimum PPE required (safety shoes, high-visibility vest, safety glasses) before entering any operational area. Not permitted in high-hazard areas without SHEE Head approval and designated technical escort. Records maintained in Visitor Register (L4-IMS-800-RC-01).
§8Management of Change at Operational Level 5 Triggers · MoC Register WS7 · Plant Manager Approval

Before any planned change to operational processes, equipment, materials, working methods, or safety-critical personnel, an MoC assessment is completed per L2-IMS-600-P-03 §14 and recorded in the MoC Register (WS7).

Operational MoC Triggers
🔥 Changes to furnace operating parameters beyond defined ranges
⚗ Introduction of new chemicals or raw materials
🔒 Modification of existing PTW or LOTO procedures
⚙️ Changes to production line layouts or equipment
⚠️ Temporary operational arrangements that deviate from documented controls
The MoC assessment identifies: updated HIRA and SEA Register requirements · updated energy plans if SEUs are affected · updated OCS content if operating criteria change · training requirements · trial period and effectiveness review timescale. Changes may not become operationally active until the Plant Manager approves the MoC and the IMS Coordinator confirms all documentation updates are complete.

§10Product and Service Release Authority 5 Product Stages · QA Manager Final Release

No product shall be released to a customer until all planned inspection and verification activities are complete and documented, and the release has been authorised by the QA Manager or designated deputy. Traceability is maintained from furnace batch through to pallet shipment.

§12Risks and Opportunities Detailed treatment → L2-IMS-600-P-03

Detailed risk treatment managed through L2-IMS-600-P-03 and the IMS Consolidated Risk Register (WS3).

🔴 Operational Risks

  • Operational controls not implemented consistently across all shifts or by contractors
  • MoC not triggered for incremental changes that cumulatively create significant uncontrolled risk
  • Product released without all inspection records complete
  • Incoming materials accepted without required conformance documentation
  • OCS documents not updated following process changes or incident findings

🟢 Operational Opportunities

  • Consistent OCS implementation across all five furnaces building a strong audit evidence base for TÜV Austria Stage 2
  • Contractor safety integration raising overall site safety performance
  • Lean documentation — three OCS documents covering all Clause 8 requirements — reducing training burden while maintaining full ISO compliance
  • Operational energy controls delivering measurable SHC improvements and contributing to SAGCO's GHG intensity reduction target
