Working Group 1 — Plant & Quality

Chair / KPI Owner: QA Manager Meeting: Embedded in existing Plant Manager weekly meeting No new meeting required

Scope

  • ISO 9001 Quality Management System
  • Production performance and process control
  • Product conformity and heldware monitoring
  • Customer satisfaction and complaints management
  • Supplier evaluation and approved supplier list

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitor quality KPIs and production performance
  • Review customer complaints and claims
  • Monitor product conformity — heldware and pack-to-melt
  • Maintain Approved Supplier List (ASL)
  • Support continual improvement initiatives
  • Report monthly to IMS Coordinator

Applicable Standards

ISO 9001:2015 ISO 9001 §8.4 Supplier Control
