Air Emissions Monitoring — the Air half of the planned "Water & Waste Data" scope item (the Water half is live as the Water Quality Lab Register). Three annual-campaign registers: Stack Emissions (particulate only — that's all US EPA Method 3A/5 ever reports at the chimney), Boundary Air Quality (6 ambient parameters, checked against the same national limits regardless of where the station sits), and Noise Monitoring (Day/Night boundary levels). Every status shown is computed live from the stored numbers — never saved as a fixed flag — and a reading at 80% of its limit or higher shows Warning even though it still passes.
