HSE-FRM-14 · ISO 45001 §8.2 / MHRSD First Aid Regulations — First Aid Kit Register (physical kit/box locations across the plant) and the digitised Monthly Inspection & Supply Check: every kit's 14-item supply list verified against its minimum required quantity, with shortages flagged and restocked. Separate from the First Aid Treatment Log (which records injuries treated and first-aider certifications, not kit contents). Governed by OC-01 §A.17.
⚠ Doc code for this specific register has not been assigned/confirmed — shown below as "L4-SHEE-PR-TBD" pending confirmation; no prior placeholder page existed to recover a number from, unlike Scaffold/Fire Pump/Crane. ⚠ The source form's kit-ID numbering skips "...010, 011, 013, 014..." — #012 is missing from the printed form itself; reproduced as-is rather than silently renumbered. ⚠ The form's own "Total Required" column (a per-item total across all kits) is a form-level summary, not kit-specific data, and isn't modeled as a separate field here — it's derivable from the register at any time if needed.
